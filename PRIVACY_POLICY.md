# Privacy Policy for Offline PDF

**Last Updated: July 4, 2026**

## Introduction

Offline PDF Komprimierer & Merger ("the App") is committed to protecting your
privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data.

## Data Collection

**We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.**

The App operates entirely offline on your device. All PDF processing (compression and merging) happens locally without any internet connection.

## Permissions

### Storage Access

The App does not request broad storage permissions. Android's system document
picker grants access only to PDF files that you explicitly select. Generated
files are written to an app-specific document directory or a location selected
by you.

**Important**: 
- Files are only accessed when you explicitly select them
- No files are accessed, modified, or scanned without your action
- No data from your files is sent to any server or third party
- App files and PDF cache data are excluded from Android cloud backup and
  device-to-device transfer

## Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not create user accounts and does not store user data on
developer-controlled servers.

PDF outputs remain only in the location selected by you. During processing, the
App may keep temporary files and a small app-private cache on your device so
compression results can be reused briefly. Cache entries expire after 24 hours
and the cache is limited in size. Android may also remove temporary app files.

You can delete locally stored app data at any time by clearing the App's storage
in Android settings or by uninstalling the App. This removes the App-private
cache and temporary files. Files that you explicitly saved outside app-private
storage remain under your control and can be deleted from your file manager.

## Data Processing

All PDF operations are performed locally on your device:
- PDF compression uses a local Ghostscript backend when available
- PDF merging combines files on your device
- No file content leaves your device
- No internet connection is required or used

## Third-Party Services

The App does NOT:
- Use analytics services
- Include advertisements
- Connect to any servers
- Share data with third parties
- Use tracking technologies

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages as it does not collect any personal information.

## Data Security

Since the App processes files entirely on your device and does not transmit any
data, your files remain under your control. The Android build does not request
internet access and blocks cleartext network traffic at the application level.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.
